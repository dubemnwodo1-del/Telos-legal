Telos Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

1. Who we are

Telos is a journaling, habits, goals, calendar, and personal-reflection app provided by Dubem Nwodo (“Telos”, “we”, “us”, or “our”). If you have a privacy question, contact us at telos.tracker@gmail.com.

2. Information we handle

Telos is designed to work locally on your device. Depending on the features you choose, we may handle:

  • Account information: email address, authentication details, and an account identifier when you create or sign into an account.
  • Your Telos content: journal entries, check-ins and answers, moods, habits, goals, tasks, calendar events, preferences, themes, and AI conversations.
  • Optional voice input: audio is processed by your device’s speech-recognition service to create dictated text. Telos uses the resulting text; it does not intentionally retain raw microphone recordings.
  • Optional integrations: access tokens and the calendar, task, or Notion information you choose to connect, import, sync, or export.
  • Purchase and subscription information: purchase status and entitlement information supplied by the applicable app store and purchase service. We do not receive your full payment-card details.
  • Diagnostics and advertising information: crash/error data and device or advertising information that may be collected by Sentry and Google AdMob when those services are active.

3. How we use information

We use information to provide and improve Telos, save and synchronize data you choose to back up, deliver reminders, process purchases, provide customer support, protect the service, diagnose failures, and show rewarded advertisements where available.

If you use Telos AI, relevant content from your Telos data—such as journal text, check-ins, habits, goals, tasks, and questions—may be sent through our secure server to the selected AI provider to generate the response you requested. Do not use AI features for emergencies or as medical, mental-health, legal, or financial advice.

4. When information is shared

We share information only as needed to run the features you choose. This can include:

  • Supabase for authentication, cloud backup, and server functions.
  • Our AI provider when you request an AI chat or insight.
  • Sentry for crash and error reporting.
  • Google AdMob for rewarded advertisements.
  • Google Calendar, Google Tasks, or Notion only when you connect and use the relevant integration.
  • Apple App Store, Google Play, and purchase providers to process and restore purchases.

We do not sell your journal entries or other Telos content.

5. Your choices and controls

You can use available settings to control reminders, cloud backup, public-profile options, integrations, and optional permissions. You can decline microphone or speech-recognition permission; voice dictation will then be unavailable. You may export data where that feature is available.

6. Data retention and deletion

Data stored only on your device remains there until you delete it or uninstall the app. If you create an account, you can initiate permanent deletion in Telos under Settings → Delete account. This deletes the account and associated cloud data. You can also request deletion through our public deletion page at https://dubemnwodo1-del.github.io/Telos-legal/delete-account.html.

Some information may be retained only where necessary for legal obligations, fraud prevention, security, or resolving disputes. Store subscriptions are managed by the relevant app store and must be cancelled there separately.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

Telos is not directed to children under the age required to consent to data processing in their jurisdiction. Do not use Telos if you are not old enough to do so under applicable law.

9. International processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live. Those countries may have different data-protection laws.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will post the revised version here with a new “Last updated” date. Questions or requests can be sent to telos.tracker@gmail.com.
