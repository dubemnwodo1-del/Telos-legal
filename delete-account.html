Delete your Telos account

This page explains how to permanently delete your Telos account and associated data.

Deletion is permanent. Your journal entries, check-ins, habits, goals, tasks, calendar events, cloud backups, and account data will be deleted. This cannot be undone.

Delete from the Telos app

  1. Open Telos and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Delete account and confirm the deletion.

The account and associated cloud data are deleted when the request succeeds. Data stored locally on the device is also removed by the app.

Unable to sign in?

If you cannot access the app, email telos.tracker@gmail.com with the subject “Telos account deletion request” and the email address associated with your account. We may request enough information to verify that you own the account before completing the request.

Subscriptions

Deleting your Telos account does not cancel an active subscription billed by Apple App Store or Google Play. Cancel subscriptions through the relevant app-store account settings to prevent future charges.

Questions

For questions about deletion or retained information, contact telos.tracker@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
